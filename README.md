# 🛠️ Project Box

<!-- Photo officiel de la Project Box -->

Welcome to the official repository for the **Project Box**. 
This project is designed to provide a versatile, professional-grade enclosure for your DIY builds, such as:
 * Home Automation: Home Assistant, ESPHome, WLED
 * Outdoor Automation: LEDs, water pumps, actuators
 * Robotics: Arduino, ESP32, motor drivers
 * Aquariums & Terrariums: Climate and lighting control
 * ...and much more.

<!-- Cas d'usage IA -->

> [!WARNING]
> **SAFETY NOTICE - STRICTLY LOW VOLTAGE (MAX 48V)**
> This enclosure is engineered and intended **ONLY for Low Voltage applications (12V, 24V, 48V)**. It is NOT certified, tested, or designed to handle mains voltage (110V/220V AC). 
>  **Do not route mains power through this box.**

---

## ✨ Main Features

**Generic Version:**

* 🧱 **Material:** 3D printed in PETG-CF (Carbon Fiber) for exceptional durability, heat tolerance, and UV resistance.
* 🧩 **Modular Design:** Includes 2 interchangeable internal baseplates to easily mount generic ESP32/Arduino boards, motor drivers, or actuators.
* 💧 **Weather & Dust Resistance:** The top cover is sealed with a custom EPDM gasket, paired with M20 cable glands for perfect mechanical strain relief and splash protection.
* 🛠️ **Flexible Mounting:** Install the Project Box anywhere using the rear sliding rail mechanism. Slide the box in or out in seconds for easy maintenance.
* 🔗 **Cable Management:** Features built-in anchor points on the interior walls to secure cables with zip-ties for a clean, professional wiring job.

<!-- Capture CAO + légende -->

**Raspberry Pi Option:**

* 🍓 **Raspberry Pi Compatible:** Dedicated mounting for your Raspberry Pi with direct access to all I/O ports. *(Note: Due to ventilation, this version is not waterproof).*
* 🧩 **Hybrid Layout:** Retains 1 interchangeable baseplate to mount an additional ESP32/Arduino board or actuator alongside your Pi.
* 💨 **Active Cooling:** Built-in 40x40mm fan mount equipped with a front dust mesh filter for optimal airflow.

<!-- Capture CAO + légende -->

**Relay Module Option:**

* ⚡ **Relay Compatibility:** Custom-fit for the standard [ELEGOO 8-Channel 5V DC Relay Module (with optocouplers)](https://wiki.elegoo.com/oshw-parts-&-accessories/8-CHANNEL?ref=blog.shwok.com). We strongly recommend to buy the genuine version for perfect compatibility.
* 🛡️ **Mechanical Protection:** Features an integrated snap-on cover to hide cables, secure the wiring, and prevent accidental contact during maintenance.

## 🚀 Assembly & Installation Guide for Generic version

### 1. Mounting the Electronics
* **ESP32 / Generic Board Users**: Clean the bottom of the enclosure. Peel off the backing of the provided adhesive nylon standoffs, place them according to your board's footprint, and simply snap your PCB into place.

<!-- Photo exemple avec ESP32 + légende -->
<!-- GIF insertion module -->

### 2. Cable Routing & Cable Glands
To ensure mechanical strain relief and keep the box sealed against dust:
* **Use Round Cables**: Cable glands are designed to clamp down on a single, round outer jacket between 6mm to 12mm (e.g., multi-conductor cable like Ethernet or 4G1.5mm2). 
* **Individual Wires**: If you must route multiple loose wires (like jumper wires) through a single gland, use a piece of **heat-shrink tubing** around the bundle where it passes through the gland.
* **Unused Glands**: Always install the provided **blanking plugs** in any unused cable glands.

<!-- Photo Gland OK et Photo Gland KO + légende -->

### 3. Internal Wiring
* Route your Dupont / signal cables neatly. 
* Use small zip-ties on the internal anchor points (bridges) to secure the wiring harness *before* it connects to the GPIO pins or relays. This prevents accidental pull-outs.

<!-- Photo intérieur box + légende -->

### 4. Wall Mounting
Use the rear mounting holes to secure the enclosure to your wall. Make sure to mount the box with the cable glands facing **downwards** to prevent dust or moisture accumulation.

## 🚀 Assembly & Installation Guide for option Raspberry

### 1. Mounting the Electronics
* **Raspberry Pi Users**: Secure your Pi (3, 4, or 5) using the M2.5 screws. Do not overtighten to avoid stressing the PCB. Go to [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/) for more information.
* **Fan Users**: Secure your Pi 40mmx40mm fan using the M3 screws. Do not overtighten.

## 🚀 Assembly & Installation Guide for option Relay board

### 1. Mounting the Electronics
* **Relay board Users**: Secure the standard ELEGOO 8-Channel 5V DC Relay Module (with optocouplers) using the M3 screws. Do not overtighten to avoid stressing the PCB. Go to [ELEGOO Wiki](https://wiki.elegoo.com/oshw-parts-&-accessories/8-CHANNEL?ref=blog.shwok.com) for more information.
* **Fan Users**: Secure your Pi 40mmx40mm fan using the M3 screws. Do not overtighten.

<!-- Photo intérieur box + légende -->

### 2. Internal Wiring
* Route your Dupont / signal cables neatly. 
* Use small zip-ties on the internal anchor points (bridges) to secure the wiring harness *before* it connects to the GPIO pins or relays. This prevents accidental pull-outs.
* **Power Supply:** We highly recommend using the official power supply of your Raspberry Pi. It provides sufficient headroom to power both the Pi and the 8-channel relay module simultaneously without stability issues.

<!-- Schéma câblage -->

### 3. Cable Routing & Cable Glands
To ensure mechanical strain relief and keep the box sealed against dust:
* **Use Round Cables**: Cable glands are designed to clamp down on a single, round outer jacket between 6mm to 12mm (e.g., multi-conductor cable like 4G1.5). 
* **Individual Wires**: If you must route multiple loose wires through a single gland, use a piece of **heat-shrink tubing** around the bundle where it passes through the gland. Use zip-ties to secure wires to the relay board's anchor points to prevent mechanical strain on the connections.
* **Unused Glands**: Always install the provided **blanking plugs** in any unused cable glands.
* Once the relays are wired, install the **Relay Protection Cover** using the dedicated M3 screws.

## 🛠️ 3D Files

Customize your box! In the `3D_Files` folder of this repository, you will find:

*   **`generic_module.step`**: An interchangeable blank internal baseplate. Import this file into Fusion360, FreeCAD, or TinkerCAD to design custom mounts for your specific hardware (e.g., custom PCBs, buck converters, or sensors).
*   **`Free_Box_1m_noOption.stl`**: A free, basic 1-module version of the Project Box. If you need a simple, rugged enclosure without the advanced modular plates, you can download it here or visit the [Printables Box](https://www.printables.com/model/1813337-rugged-weather-resistant-box).
